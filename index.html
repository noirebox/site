Capabilities

Proof, not promises.

Nothing here asks for trust. Every claim on this page maps to a mechanism you can inspect, replay, or break on purpose.

01Tamper-evident by construction

Every append is sealed into a SHA-256 hash chain. Change one byte anywhere in the history and the chain breaks — visibly, on replay. No configuration, no option to disable.

02Witnessed from the outside

Ed25519 signatures, RFC 3161 multi-TSA and OpenTimestamps (Bitcoin). The record is anchored to something nobody — including the operator — can rewrite later.

03Verifiable offline

A single stdlib-only Python file or a plain browser page replays the whole chain. No server, no account, no trust required. verify.py, take it or leave it.

04Compliance, mapped line by line

GDPR art. 5(2)/15/20, AI Act art. 12, 14(5), 50(2), 55(1)(c), 73, eIDAS art. 41 — each requirement mapped to a shipped feature in COMPLIANCE-EU.md. Undecidable states stay named; they are never folded into a green check.
