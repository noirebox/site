Proof, not promises.

Nothing here asks for trust. Every claim on this page maps to a mechanism you can inspect, replay or break on purpose.

01Tamper-evident by construction

Every append is sealed into a SHA-256 hash chain. Change one byte anywhere in the history and the chain breaks — visibly, on replay.

02Signed & time-stamped

Ed25519 signatures, RFC 3161 multi-TSA and OpenTimestamps (Bitcoin). The record is witnessed by the outside world, not by the operator.

03Verifiable offline

A single Python file or a plain browser page replays the whole chain. No server, no account, no trust required — verify.py is stdlib-only.

04Compliance, mapped

GDPR art. 5(2)/15/20, AI Act art. 12, 14(5), 50(2), 55(1)(c), 73, eIDAS art. 41 — each requirement mapped to a shipped feature in COMPLIANCE-EU.md.

Seal. Witness. Verify.

Three moves, in this order, every time the agent acts.

STEP 1

Seal

Your agent appends every decision to a hash-chained journal — 0.17 ms per append, in process, no network round-trip needed.

STEP 2

Witness

Signatures and external timestamps anchor the record to something nobody can rewrite later — not even the operator.

STEP 3

Verify

Anyone replays the chain offline. A broken link is proof of tampering; an intact chain is proof of integrity. Undecidable states stay named, never folded into a green check.
