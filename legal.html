// legal & privacy

The compliance starts here.

We sell proof and compliance — so this page is not an afterthought. There is no cookie banner on this site, because there is nothing to consent to. What the law does require is below: who publishes, who hosts, what is (not) collected, and your rights.

01Publisher

SL
SAM LABBES//ABB — independent developer · Vosges, France

Directeur de publication : Sam LABBE. Contact : sam@slabb.dev

noirebox.eu is a non-commercial website presenting the open-source NoireBox project (the flight data recorder for AI agents). There is no e-commerce, no paid account and no newsletter on this site.

02Personal data — what this site does NOT collect

  • Zero cookies — no consent banner is needed when there is nothing to consent to.
  • Zero tracking — no analytics, no ads, no social pixels, no fingerprinting.
  • Zero personal data processed by visiting this site.
  • One script, and it stays local — the only JavaScript on this page is the “copy” button next to the install command. It copies text to your clipboard and never sends anything anywhere.

03Email — the only channel

If you write to us, your email address and message are used solely to answer you (legal basis: legitimate interest / pre-contractual steps). They are kept only as long as needed and are never sold, rented or shared.

04Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), fronted by Cloudflare for DNS and protection. Hosting logs may be kept by these providers for security purposes, under their own policies and applicable safeguards.

05Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability — write to us and it is handled without bureaucracy:

sam@slabb.dev

You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French supervisory authority.

06Open source — verified, not promised

NoireBox is MIT-licensed and everything is public: github.com/noirebox/noirebox. The claims on the home page point at artifacts (benchmarks, CI, ADRs, the EU compliance mapping) — not at assertions about artifacts.

The companion blog at blog.noirebox.eu goes further: every page is hash-chained and Ed25519-sealed, and anyone can verify it offline at blog.noirebox.eu/verify.html.
