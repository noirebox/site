01 / Architecture & trust

Proof, not promises.

Nothing here asks for trust. Every claim on this page maps to an artifact you can inspect, replay, or break on purpose — the inspector above is the product, running live.

01

Tamper-evident by construction

Every sealed append extends a SHA-256 hash chain. Change one byte anywhere in the history and the chain breaks — visibly, on replay. No configuration, no option to disable.

H(n) = SHA256( H(n-1) ‖ SHA256(bytes_n) )
02

Witnessed from the outside

Ed25519 signatures, RFC 3161 multi-TSA and OpenTimestamps (Bitcoin). The record is anchored to something nobody — including the operator — can rewrite later.

RFC 8032 Ed25519 • RFC 3161 • OpenTimestamps → Bitcoin L1
03

Verifiable offline, forever

Auditors replay any record on an air-gapped laptop: verify.py is stdlib-only, the browser page above is dependency-free. Zero network calls to verify, zero dependency on NoireBox infrastructure.

$ python3 verify.py https://blog.noirebox.eu
04

An honest scope, mapped to EU law

NoireBox proves a record was not altered and when it existed. It does not prove the record was true when written — undecidable is a first-class outcome, never folded into a green check. Every EU requirement is mapped to a shipped feature in COMPLIANCE-EU.md.
