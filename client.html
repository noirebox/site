The rule, stated plainly

Europe now requires proof — not checkboxes.

The EU AI Act requires automatic logging of every decision your AI makes (art. 12). A log file is not proof: anyone with the disk can edit it. NoireBox makes the record impossible to rewrite silently — that is what turns an obligation into evidence.

Sources: AI Act art. 12 · GDPR art. 5(2) · the full mapping is public — COMPLIANCE-EU.md
